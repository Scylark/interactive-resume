The idea

A cycling training app built to make you stronger on the bike

WattPlan builds your training around your goals and the time you have, adjusting it as things change. The tagline carries the whole product in two words, and it reads four ways.

The cyclist trains

The rider does the work.

The body adapts

Training changes what the rider can do.

The plan adapts

It follows the riding that was actually done.

The loop

Train, adapt, and go again.

What the rider should feel

Confidence that their training is right. This is the emotional benefit the brand works towards. It guides the copy and stays out of it.

Creative direction

Nostalgic, filtered, in motion

WattPlan photography and film share one look. It reads like a roll of film from a hard ride, and it is made to sit behind a dark app with one bright accent.

Nostalgic

Grain, light leaks and soft edges. A frame should feel remembered.

Filtered

Colour leans to dusk blue and purple with the highlights held back, so volt stays the brightest thing on screen.

Motion blur

The background and the wheels streak. The rider stays readable.

Voice

An experienced club rider who knows the science

The Coach sets the voice for the whole brand. If a line would sound wrong coming from the rider next to you on a Sunday, it is wrong on the page too.

Know the science, skip the medicine

Sound like someone who has read the research and ridden the miles. Keep medical language out.

Warm and direct

Friendly, plain, and with room for dry humour.

One clear answer

Recommend something. A rider who asks what to do tomorrow wants a decision.

Tomorrow becomes a rest day because you need to absorb this block.

More useful than telling the rider they have a few options.

Stop when it is said

Finish when the answer is complete. Cut the throat-clearing and the repetition.

Claims that never ship

Guaranteed gains, promised watts, FTP points, times or placings, medical or safety claims, and any comparison of the Coach with a human coach.

Messaging house

Every line traces to a pillar

The house was built from the bottom up. The proof came first, with each claim mapped to shipped code, and the pillars and the tagline were layered on top. A line that cannot be traced to a pillar does not ship. How it was built is on the Manual Focus blog.

The week

Personalised. Training

When life or riding changes, your week still makes sense.

  • The plan is built around the days and hours you can ride.
  • Missed workouts are repaired without crowding hard days.
  • Harder or easier rides influence what comes next.
The Coach

Questions. Answered.

Ask a normal question and get a clear answer based on your plan, rides and goals.

  • Answers use your plan, recent rides, availability and event.
  • Ask the Coach to skip, move or reduce a workout.
  • Goals, injuries and preferences carry across conversations.
The data

Rides. Counted.

Connect once, then ride. WattPlan imports the file, scores it and spots what changed.

  • Garmin, Strava and Wahoo rides sync automatically.
  • Each file updates Weighted Power, Intensity and Load.
  • Personal bests and possible FTP changes are flagged.
