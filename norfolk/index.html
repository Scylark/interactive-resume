The one thing that governs the week

Low tide marches right

Each bar is one afternoon, 06:00 to 21:00. The gold band is the workable window either side of low water, when the reef at West Runton is exposed and the fossils are findable.

06:0011:0016:0021:00

These are extrapolated, not looked up. Worked forward from Sunday's low water at Cromer, so treat them as give or take twenty minutes and confirm on the day. There's a second low each morning too, drifting from about 01:40 on Monday to 07:30 by the last day, which is too early to be useful until the weekend.

Crab and bread

Picked up as you pass through Sheringham or Cromer, usually the morning of the day you eat it. Dressed crab, a decent loaf, mayonnaise, lemon, and something green. That's lunch most days and it doesn't need planning beyond remembering to stop.
