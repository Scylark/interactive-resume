Our mission

Project Echelon is a professional cycling team that educates, equips, and empowers veterans and their communities through physical activity and self-discovery.

How the team and the non-profit fit

They run as one organization. The racing brings in the partners who back our veterans. The veterans give the racing its purpose.

Project Echelon was founded on April 1, 2016 by Eric Beach and Eric Hill. Its home is Waukesha, Wisconsin.

The name

In a crosswind, riders form a diagonal line called an echelon. Each rider shelters the next. New members start in the shelter of the line, matched with one of our pro riders.
