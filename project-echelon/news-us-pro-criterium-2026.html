Elphingstone wins the US Pro criterium

June 19, 2026. Charleston, West Virginia.

On this page

  • Race report
  • Result

Race report

Luke Elphingstone won the 2026 men's US Pro criterium championship in downtown Charleston. It's the team's second elite men's national title. Luke now wears the Stars and Stripes jersey.

An early break got clear and Caleb Classen rode hard in it to set Luke up. Behind them, the rest of the team controlled the chase. Luke won the sprint.
