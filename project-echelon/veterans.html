For veterans

Veterans and service members can join. You'll be matched with one of our pro riders.

Veterans Crisis LineIf you or a veteran you know is in crisis, dial 988, then press 1. You can also text 838255. It's free and confidential, 24 hours a day.

How joining works

This is the first month, step by step.

  1. Contact formTell us a little about yourself.
  2. Welcome emailYou get resources to read and a few first tasks.
  3. Goal settingWork through our goal-setting course. Then fill in a reflective survey and an interest inventory.
  4. Meet your riderWe put you in touch with one of our pro riders, based on where you live.
  5. OnboardingYou and your rider meet and plan the next steps around your goals.
  6. The communityYou get our Google Classroom and member benefits from our partners. You're also invited to our private Facebook group.
  7. Monthly MusterYour first group call will likely be our Monthly Muster, held online.

What we do

Educate

We're expanding coach training to better support veterans, including suicide prevention training with certified medical professionals.

Equip

Bikes and scholarships get veterans started. Miles on a Mission brings them to international cycling events.

Empower

Cycling gives veterans a routine. The team gives them people to ride with.

What support can cover

Every veteran's needs are different. A coach gets to know you first, then we work out what would help.

Equipment

  • A bicycle or an indoor trainer
  • A helmet or wheels
  • Cycling kit, running kit or a triathlon suit

Memberships and race fees

  • USA Cycling or USA Triathlon membership
  • Race entry
  • A gym or class
  • TrainingPeaks
  • A training camp for your sport

Ask us if something you need isn't listed.

Monthly Muster

Members meet online once a month. It's likely to be your first group call.
