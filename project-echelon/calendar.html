About each race

Southern California

Late January. California. The season starts with a training camp at the end of January. It is the new roster's first block of riding together.

Valley of the Sun Stage Race

Feb 12 to 14. Phoenix, Arizona. The 35th edition opens the American season. A time trial in Buckeye comes first, then a road race on a flat loop south of Phoenix. A criterium settles it on overall time.

Tucson Bicycle Classic

Feb 18 to 21. Tucson, Arizona. Four days of racing around Tucson, with a time trial on standard road bikes. Brendan Rhim won it overall for the team in 2024. Luke Elphingstone and Ben Boroff each won a stage in 2026.

Croatia

March. Istria, Croatia. In 2025 the European spring began in Istria, at the Poreč Classic and the Istrian Spring Tour. The 2027 race is still to be confirmed.

Spain

Race to be confirmed. Spain. Earlier seasons took the team to Challenge Mallorca and the Volta a la Comunitat Valenciana. The 2027 race in Spain is still to be named.

Portugal

Race to be confirmed. Portugal. The team has raced the Volta ao Alentejo, a five-day UCI stage race across the south of the country. The 2027 race is still to be confirmed.

Redlands Bicycle Classic

April. Redlands, California. First held in 1985, a year after the Los Angeles Olympics. Project Echelon won it three years running, with Tyler Stites from 2022 to 2024.

Battenkill

May 22. Cambridge, New York. A one-day race in Washington County with about a fifth of it on dirt roads. Its organizers compare it to the Belgian classics. In 2027 it returns to the UCI calendar for the first time since 2012.

Tour of Norway

May 27 to 30. Norway. The highest-ranked race on the team's calendar. The 2026 edition was cancelled. The 2027 race depends on state funding coming back.

Armed Forces Cycling Classic

June 5 to 6. Washington, DC, and Arlington, Virginia. Saturday's Capitol Cup in Washington joins the UCI calendar in 2027. Sunday is the Clarendon Cup criterium in Arlington, held since 1998. Stephen Vogel won it for the team in 2021.

Philadelphia Cycling Classic

Aug 22. Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Laps of a 14-mile circuit that climbs the Manayunk Wall, which hits 17 percent. The race began in 1985 as the US pro championship. It came back in 2026 after nine years away, with Colby Lange in the early break.

Follow the racing

In 2026 you could watch on FloSports or Eurosport. Peacock and ABC showed races too. Cyclingnews carried the reports.
