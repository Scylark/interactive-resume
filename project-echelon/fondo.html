Project Echelon Fondo

A charity ride near Tucson with the pro team. It's a social ride, so nobody is racing.

On this page

  • About the ride
  • Registration
  • Where to stay

About the ride

Veterans and their families ride with the pro team and local cyclists. The route uses open roads around Oro Valley and Marana, so everyone follows traffic laws.

Both routes stop at two veteran memorials in Pima County. Each stop is time to regroup and reflect.

Registration

Veterans get a discount. Answer the custom questions when you register to claim it.
