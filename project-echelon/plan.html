How the new site works

My answers to your feedback of November 22. It covers what changed, where everything lives and how you edit it.

On this page

  • What your feedback changed
  • What your traffic shows
  • One site instead of two
  • How you edit it

What your feedback changed

Your feedback and what changed
You saidWhat I've done
Returning visitors can't get straight to thingsThe full menu now stays at the top of every page, on phones as well as laptops. In the last version it disappeared on smaller screens. That was a mistake on my side.
The story should be the home page onlyIt is. Everything else is a plain page that doesn't move. There are 15 of them, plus a page for each rider.
People need a clear way out of the story"Skip the story" is always on screen. Next to it is a link to the plain page for whatever you're looking at.
Veterans with TBIOne button turns motion off and the site remembers it. Plain pages never move. "Larger text" is in the menu.
Screen readersEvery page has one main heading and proper labels. All of it works from the keyboard.
Your players couldn't find things when askedThere's a search, plus an A to Z of the whole site. Please run the same test with them again.

One site instead of two

I agree with you. I'd bring them together at projectechelon.org and point every page of projectechelonracing.com at its new home. The .org carries your news archive and it's the address donors will trust. Nobody loses a bookmark, because each old address sends people to the right new page.

How you edit it

You sign in, change the words in a box and press Publish. The site updates within a couple of minutes. You can't break the layout, because the design isn't part of what you edit.

Try the editing screen
The jobs you listed
JobWhat you doWhere it changes
Change a sponsor codeOpen Sponsors and codes, choose the sponsor, type the code, press PublishThe codes page and the musette
Add or end a sponsorPress Add sponsor and drop in the logo. To end one, switch it offThe partner page, the codes page and the home page
End a code on a dateSet its last day onceIt comes off by itself
Add or move a raceOpen Race calendar, change the date or the status, press PublishThe calendar and the season section on the home page
Post news or PRPress Add post and paste your text. For an article elsewhere, paste its linkThe news page and the home page
Post a resultAdd it to the race report and choose the riderThe results page and that rider's page
Update a riderOpen Riders, change the line or the photo, press PublishThe roster, the rider's page and the home page

The editing screen above is a mock-up. The real one is Sanity, which has the same boxes. It should cost you nothing, because Sanity has a free plan and a non-profit plan. The account would be in Echelon's name.

Layout changes or a brand new kind of page need a developer. My two free days a year cover that.
