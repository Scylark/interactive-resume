01

Countdown

LEAD LINEThe Pyramid Black Friday Sale. Every product, up to 30% off.

The whole bike, full bleed, with a date block that tightens as the sale closes: the end date at launch, then days left, then last day. The sale feels like an event with a clear end.

For: obvious urgency, easy daily swaps. Against: the most "Black Friday" of the four, so it looks like everyone else's.

02

Black Friday tape

LEAD LINEBlack Friday at Pyramid. Every product, up to 30% off.

A black band of repeating mono type, "BLACK FRIDAY · UP TO 30% OFF", wraps across every asset like tape on a shop window. The bike stays the hero; the tape says sale before anyone reads a word. Each day only the tape changes: "LAST CHANCE · LAST CHANCE".

For: unmistakably Black Friday, instantly recognisable across every channel, and the daily swap is one line of text. Against: the tape can cover the part we want to show, so crops need planning around it.

The number

LEAD LINESave up to 30%. Every product.

Type-led. A huge outlined 30 in Space Mono does the work, with the bike underneath as texture. Reads in half a second on a phone, so it is built for the feed and paid social.

For: the fastest read and the strongest stop in the scroll. Against: leads with price, the thing Pyramid normally never does, and only tier D products get the full 30%.

04

Blackout

LEAD LINEBlack Friday, in black and white.

Takes "Black Friday" literally. The bike comes out of a black background, lit like a studio shot, with small centred type and a lot of space. Premium, calm and very different from the shouting in everyone's inbox.

For: the most distinctive, and it makes the black and white rule the idea itself. Against: needs the best photography of the four, and the offer can get lost if the type is too small.

OUR STEER

Lead with 02 Black Friday tape. It says Black Friday at a glance on every channel, keeps the bike as the hero, and the daily change is one line of text, which suits a team of four. 04 Blackout is the more premium alternative if Adam B wants Pyramid to stand apart from the noise. Either way, keep 03's big number for Meta ads only.
